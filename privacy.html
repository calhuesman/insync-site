InSync Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 28, 2026

InSync ("InSync," "we," "us") is an iOS app that helps you decide what to cook, with a partner or on your own. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. If you have questions, contact us at insyncapp.help@gmail.com.

1. Information we collect

Account information

  • Your email address, if you sign in with a one-time code sent to your email
  • Your Apple sign-in identifier and the email Apple shares with us (this may be a private relay address), if you use Sign in with Apple
  • Your Google account identifier, name, and email, if you use Sign in with Google

Subscription information

  • Whether you have InSync+, your plan, and its trial, renewal, and end dates. Apple handles payment; we never see your card details
  • Whether your account has been given InSync+ at no charge (a "comped" account). We keep a short list of comped accounts, linked to the account.

Profile information you give us

  • Display name
  • A profile photo, if you add one
  • Diet rules and allergies you choose (for example vegetarian, gluten-free, or peanut-free)
  • Foods you want to avoid
  • Your onboarding answers, for example who you usually cook with and for how many people, your cooking skill, how spicy you like food, how long you like to spend cooking, the kitchen gear you have, which meals you usually cook (breakfast, lunch, or dinner), your favorite foods and cuisines, how adventurous you are, your goals, and your weekly meal goal
  • Your notification settings and the time you choose for the daily "Craving check" reminder

Diet rules, allergies, and foods to avoid are optional. They may reveal health-related information, such as a food allergy. We use them only to filter meal suggestions for you and anyone you cook with in InSync.

Activity in the app

  • Your cravings and swipes for each round, and your plan for it (the meal, time, goal, effort, and any fridge items, leftovers, or occasion you add)
  • Match results, meals you propose and pick, cooking progress, ratings, and notes
  • Your cooked-meal history, streaks, weekly goal progress, stats, and achievements (worked out from the meals you cook)
  • Your lists: favorites, To Cook, "never again," and "make again soon"
  • How you use InSync: paired with a partner, on one phone with a guest, or on your own ("Just me"). In one-phone mode we store the guest's name, food rules, foods to avoid, spice preference, ratings, and a guest photo if you add one

Photos

  • Meal photos and profile photos you choose to add. We only access the camera or photo library when you tap to add a photo, and only the photos you pick. Photos are kept in private storage, not public links.

Information kept only on your phone

  • Before you sign in, your onboarding answers, a profile photo you picked, and any meal you save with "Save it for later" stay on your phone. After you sign in, they are saved to your account (the saved meal goes to your To Cook list).
  • Copies of photos, so they load quickly and work offline
  • Practice ("demo") rounds with a pretend partner, and your reminder schedule

Device and usage information

  • A push notification token, if you allow notifications
  • App version and basic in-app usage events (for example which screens were completed), stored in our own database to understand how the app is used and improve it

Support messages

  • Anything you send us when you contact support

2. Information we do not collect

  • We do not collect your location, contacts, or health data from Apple Health.
  • We do not show ads.
  • We do not sell your personal information or share it for advertising.
  • We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

3. How we use information

  • To run the app: sign you in, pair you with your partner (or set up one-phone or Just me use), match your cravings, suggest meals, run cook mode, and save your history, stats, and achievements
  • To filter out meals that don't fit your diet rules, allergies, foods you avoid, kitchen gear, or the meal you're planning
  • To send notifications you allow, such as when your partner picks their cravings, your match is ready, a meal is proposed, or a pick changes, and the reminders you turn on (the Craving check, your monthly recap, and your free trial ending). Some reminders are scheduled on your phone. When your partner starts a round, we may send your phone a silent notification that only removes that day's Craving check reminder
  • To keep the app secure, prevent abuse, and fix problems
  • To understand how the app is used and improve it
  • To respond to you when you contact us
  • To comply with law

4. Who can see your information

Your partner. When you pair, your partner can see your display name, your profile photo (only while you're paired), your top cravings after you both lock in (not your "not tonight" picks or individual swipes), meals you propose and pick together, cooking progress, ratings, notes, meal photos for meals you cooked together, and your shared lists. After you unpair, your former partner can still view the shared meal history from your time together, but can no longer add to it or see your profile photo.

On one phone or on your own. In one-phone mode, the guest's information stays in your account. With Just me, your activity isn't shared with anyone. If you later add a second phone with a code, the person who joins can see the history already in that account.

When you share. If you choose to share a match or recap card, it goes only where you send it through your iPhone's share sheet. Recap cards include first names only if you turn that on.

Service providers who help us run InSync, only as needed to provide their service:

  • Supabase (database, file storage, sign-in, and sign-in emails)
  • Apple (Sign in with Apple, push notifications, and App Store purchases)
  • Google (Sign in with Google), if you choose it
  • RevenueCat, which manages InSync+ subscriptions. It receives your InSync account ID, your App Store purchase details, and basic device information (such as device model and iOS version), not your recipes, cravings, or photos.

Legal reasons. We may share information if required by law, to protect the rights and safety of our users or others, or in connection with a sale or transfer of the app.

5. How long we keep information

We keep your information while your account is active. You can delete your account anytime in the app (Profile, then About, then Delete account). When you delete your account, we delete your account, profile, profile photos (including a one-phone guest's photo), the meal photos you uploaded, private picks, favorites, and push tokens. Records of meals you cooked with a partner may remain in your former partner's history without being linked to your account. Information kept only on your phone is removed when you delete the app. We may keep limited information when required by law or to prevent abuse.

6. Security

We use industry-standard safeguards, including access rules that limit each person to their own data and their current partner's shared data, and private storage for photos. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure.

7. Children

InSync is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Where information is stored

Our service providers store information in the United States. If you use InSync from outside the U.S., your information will be transferred to and processed in the U.S.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Edit your profile, profile photo, diet rules, allergies, and foods to avoid in the app
  • Choose which notifications you get in the app (Profile, then Notifications), or turn them all off in iOS Settings
  • Delete photos you uploaded, or delete your whole account, in the app
  • Request a copy of your information, or ask us to correct or delete it, by contacting us

Depending on where you live (for example California or the European Union), you may have additional rights. We will respond to requests as required by applicable law.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. If we make significant changes, we will let you know in the app. The effective date at the top shows when it was last updated.
